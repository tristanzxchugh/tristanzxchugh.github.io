About Me

I’m into the numbers.
But I care about what they actually do.

Tristan Reyes

Digital Marketing Specialist

Hey, I’m Tristan. I work on paid search campaigns, lead generation, and the tracking that tells us whether any of it is actually working.

I’m the kind of marketer who wants to know where every lead came from, what made someone click, and whether the campaign is bringing in real opportunities, not just making the dashboard look busy.

The goal is pretty simple: spend smarter, track things properly, and put more budget behind what’s actually working.

I’m big on clear reporting and straight answers. If something is performing, I’ll show you why. If it isn’t, I’ll dig into it, fix what can be fixed, and cut the stuff that’s wasting budget. No mystery metrics and no reports full of numbers with zero context.

I’m always learning, testing, and sharpening my skills as advertising platforms change, and honestly, that’s part of what I enjoy about the work.
